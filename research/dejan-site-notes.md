# Dejan's vocabulary and style (from adriaticcoaching.com, Oct 2026)

Working notes used to build `shared/glossary.json` and the translation prompt. Paraphrased; term pairs only.

## Style rules that matter for translation
- Address: informal **ti** by default. **Vi** shows up in older/business/race-week posts; treat as a register flag, not a rule.
- Gender-inclusive doubling when writing Croatian: "uporan ili uporna", "sportašica i sportaša", "krenuo/krenula". Preserve when going EN → HR.
- Short sentences, often imperative-first. Numbered lists. ALL-CAPS lead-ins.
- Calm, matter-of-fact motivation. Signature lines: "Sve je moguće!", "mirna glava, jake noge", "Manje je više", "put je važniji nego cilj".
- Method words he keeps returning to: balans, kontinuitet, strpljenje, dugoročno održiv, prilagodba, ekonomičnost.
- Spelling is loose: "sa" instead of "s" everywhere, typos (intezitet, breefing, zavislaj). Do not mimic typos in output, but recognize them in input.
- Loanwords he keeps in English inside Croatian: age group / age grouper, PRO, slot, drafting, rolling start, brick (in parentheses), fartlek, tempo, indoor/outdoor, trail, hiking, cool down, range of motion, Core, RPE, GPS, PB, DNF, T1, HIIT, bib, half (as "half distanca"), lifestyle, onboarding.
- Loanwords he does NOT use: pace, long run, taper, base/build, FTP, sweet spot, Z2. His equivalents: tempo / brzina, duga trčanja, period odmora, zona (written "zona 3", "1. i 2. zona", "ZONA 1-2", "u 1 zoni"; never Z1/Z2).
- Croatianised English with native endings: slotova, gelovi, trekeri, pulsmetar, finišer, Ironmani, kampovi, treninzi, šejk.
- Numbers: digits above ~3, words for small counts. Decimal comma in body text ("0,5 kg", "42,2 km") but dot in race labels ("70.3", "3.8 km"). Ranges with hyphen or "do" ("2-8 min", "1 do 3 sata").
- Units: space before spelled-out units ("30 minuta", "180 km"), no space in captions ("5h", "40kmh"). "ot/min" for bpm. Percent attached ("80%").
- Times: "1:59:18", "9:03"; records spelled out ("7 sati 35 min 53 sekundi"). Ordinals with period ("23. mjestu"). Dates "11. srpnja 2026." Approximations "10-tak minuta", "30-tak dana".
- Age groups "M50-54". Heart rate: "puls" (preferred over "srčani ritam"), "maksimalni puls", "puls u mirovanju".
- Run cadence: "broj koraka u minuti", target "170 i 180". Bike cadence "75-90" with no unit.
- His own English pages are calqued ("switch to feelings", "Changes at the end the week"). Reference for his intent, not a target.
