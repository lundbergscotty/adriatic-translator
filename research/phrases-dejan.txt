# Short coaching lines in Dejan's register (paraphrased from his site). Format: hr | en
Uvijek slušaj svoje tijelo. | Always listen to your body.
Ne preskači zagrijavanje. | Don't skip the warm-up.
Isteži se nakon treninga, nikada prije. | Stretch after training, never before.
Odmor je pasivan, bez kretanja. | Rest is passive, no moving around.
Odradi lakši trening ili otiđi u dugu šetnju. | Do an easier session or go for a long walk.
Ako si iscrpljen, odmor je dio napretka. | If you're wrecked, rest is part of progress.
Uvijek možeš prilagoditi intenzitet i prebaciti se na osjećaj. | You can always back off the intensity and go by feel.
Bitno je zadržati kontinuitet. | The key is consistency.
Nemoj biti nestrpljiv. | Don't be impatient.
Manje je više. | Less is more.
Tempo dio ne smije prelaziti 50% vremena trčanja toga dana. | Tempo work shouldn't be more than 50% of that day's run time.
Početnici kreću s dionicama od 1 minute. | Beginners start with 1-minute repeats.
Na brdu ćeš lakše podići intenzitet uz manje udaranja o podlogu. | Uphill you can raise intensity with less pounding.
Trčanje ograniči na maksimalno 30 minuta. | Cap the run at 30 minutes.
Nemoj trčati dan za danom. | Don't run on back-to-back days.
Drži kadencu između 170 i 180 koraka u minuti. | Keep cadence between 170 and 180 steps per minute.
Drži kukove, trup i glavu u ravnini. | Keep hips, torso and head in line.
Nauči plivati ravno, disati i orijentirati se. | Learn to swim straight, breathe and sight.
Što je voda hladnija, to sporije plivaj. | The colder the water, the slower you swim.
Drži se obale i plivaj u skupini. | Stay close to shore and swim in a group.
Pedaliranje nije samo pritiskanje pedale prema dolje. | Pedaling isn't just pushing down.
Prije svake vožnje provjeri tlak u gumama i kočnice. | Check tire pressure and brakes before every ride.
Koristi izotonik na treningu. | Use a sports drink in training.
Nemoj gladovati na treningu. | Don't go hungry in training.
Pij prije, za vrijeme i nakon treninga. | Drink before, during and after training.
Jedan trening tjedno odradi u vrućini. | Do one session a week in the heat.
Smanji trajanje treninga za 20-30%, a zadrži planirani intenzitet. | Cut the duration 20-30% but keep the planned intensity.
Postavi gornju granicu intenziteta. | Set an intensity ceiling.
Odaberi dvije utrke, jedna je uvijek rezerva. | Pick two races, one is always the backup.
Zaboravi na utrku do 48 sati prije starta. | Forget about the race until 48 hours before the start.
Odluku donesi brzo i drži je se do kraja. | Decide fast and stick with it to the end.
Nakon Ironmana savjetujem minimalno mjesec dana bez treninga. | After an Ironman I recommend at least a month off.
Plan nije Biblija, prilagođavaj ga dnevno. | The plan isn't the Bible, adjust it daily.
Mirna glava, jake noge. | Calm head, strong legs.
Uz moju podršku ćeš to razvaliti! | With my support you'll smash it!
Ne zaboravi mi javiti rezultate! | Don't forget to send me your results!
Javi se da složimo plan. | Get in touch so we can put a plan together.
Sve je moguće! | Anything is possible!
